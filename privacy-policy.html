Who we are

The App is developed and maintained by an independent developer (the "Developer"). If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the Developer at:

Email: zhilab1993@gmail.com

Data the App collects itself

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the Developer. We do not have accounts, we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any other personal information. The Developer does not receive, access, or process any of your personal data — not even the data collected by the advertising service described below, which goes directly to Google and never to the Developer.

Your game settings and preferences (such as language, selected word categories, and sound options) are stored only locally on your device and never leave it. Uninstalling the App removes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements using Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To serve ads, Google's AdMob SDK may collect and process certain information, including:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID);
  • Approximate (non-precise) location derived from your IP address;
  • Device information such as device model, operating system version, and language;
  • Information about your interaction with ads.

This data is collected directly by Google, not by the Developer. It is used to display advertising (including personalized ads where permitted) and to measure ad performance and prevent fraud.

For more information about how Google uses this data, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your consent and choices

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from within the App's privacy options, where available.

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the advertising services, please contact us so it can be addressed.
